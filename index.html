Repaso

Personal project · Next.js, PostgreSQL, Vercel

A study hub web app where I keep my interactive reviewers and notes in one place, so I can review on my laptop or my phone.

  • Desk: every reviewer in one place, with a "continue studying" row on top
  • Offline: reviewers are saved on the device, so they open without internet
  • Study stats: an attendance grid and day streaks
  • Themes: four looks, including Matcha and Coffee

My part: planned the features, designed the look, and tested every version up to 2.1.

Sign-in is limited to my own account for now, so this links to the code.

Simple Ordering System

OOP final project (group) · Java, Swing, MySQL

A desktop app for a small food shop. I built the whole Swing interface on top of a teammate's backend, without changing their logic:

  • Login: staff sign in with their email and password
  • Inventory: search, add a product, update stock, delete
  • New order: pick products into a cart, with the total updating as you go
  • Reports: total products, stock and inventory value, plus low-stock and out-of-stock counts
